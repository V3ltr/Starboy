You may be wondering… how do I put this 3D model together? 
Well, here you can find  the answer you seek!

1) Take your components and lay them out.

2) Now we will prepare the keys. You have to take the keycaps and fit them onto the switches lining up the cross.

3) Put in the PCB and any other components inside bottom of the enclosure.

4) After that, line the rim of the bottom part of the enclosure with supreglue. 

5) Then put the top on, but make sure to line them up well. Just wait and you have your enclosure.
